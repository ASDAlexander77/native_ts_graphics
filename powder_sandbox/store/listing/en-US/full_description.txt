Powder Sandbox is a falling-sand playground with real physics. Pick a material, paint it on the screen and watch what happens: water flows and levels out, sand piles up, lava sets wood alight, and fire, smoke and steam rise on their own heat.

REAL FLUIDS
Liquids don't just trickle sideways. They are incompressible: pour water into one side of a U-tube and both sides level out, as they do in a real one. Honey is thick and slow, oil floats on water, and a film of water beads up.

HEAT EVERYWHERE
Every particle and cell has a temperature. Water boils into steam and condenses again, ice melts, lava cools into stone, oil and petrol catch fire, wood and coal burn, and uranium heats up all by itself.

NEARLY 40 MATERIALS AND TOOLS
- Liquids: water, salt water, oil, petrol, honey, acid, nitroglycerin, lava
- Powders: sand, salt, gunpowder, thermite, seeds
- Gases: fire, hydrogen, oxygen, neon
- Solids: wall, glass, wood, coal, metal, ice, sponge, floating blocks, C-4
- Power: batteries, metal wires, lamps, switches, detectors, heaters, sparks and fuses
- Special: emitters, clone, fans, black holes and white holes
- Tools: heat, cool, erase, and an inspector that reads any cell's temperature and pressure

CHEMISTRY AND ELECTRICITY
Acid eats through wood, metal and stone (but not glass) and is diluted by water, salt dissolves into salt water, electricity splits water into hydrogen and oxygen, and neon glows when a current runs through it. Build circuits from batteries, wires, switches and lamps.

PLAY WITH GRAVITY
Turn gravity off, pull everything toward a point you place, or tilt your phone and let the sand slide.

CHALLENGES
Eight puzzle levels ask you to fill a cup, light a lamp, water a garden and more, with up to three stars for how fast you solve each one.

MADE FOR TOUCH
Paint with a finger, pinch to zoom, pan with two fingers, and hold still to pick a brush: circle, square, line or box. Undo a mistake, save your creation and load it later.

FAST ON YOUR PHONE
The simulation runs on the GPU through Vulkan, with up to 262,144 particles, and adjusts its grid size to your device the first time it runs.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no internet connection needed.
