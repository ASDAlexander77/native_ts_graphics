# Powder Sandbox: privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026.

Powder Sandbox is a game that runs on your device. The app has no account and no analytics or crash
reporting of its own.

- **Optional ads.** After a while of play the app asks whether you would like to watch an ad to
  support it. An ad is only shown if you choose to watch one; saying "Not now" shows nothing. The
  ads come from Google AdMob.
- **What AdMob collects.** To load and show an ad, Google's AdMob SDK connects to the internet and
  uses your device's advertising ID, your IP address and information about the device and the
  app (its model, its operating system version, how the ads are used). Google uses it to show,
  measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud, as described in
  [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
  The app itself does not read, keep or send this information.
- **Your choices.** Where the law requires it (the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland),
  the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You
  can reset or opt out of personalised ads with your advertising ID in Android's settings
  (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads).
- **Permissions.** The app asks for the internet and network-state permissions and the advertising
  ID permission, all for the ads. It reads the gravity sensor to tilt the simulation when you
  choose tilt gravity; that reading stays on the device.
- **What stays on your device.** Your saved creation, your best stars for the levels, the graphics
  quality chosen for your device and the play time until the next "support the app" question are
  kept in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app, and are not
  backed up.
- **Children.** The app is not directed at children under 13, and its ads are limited to content
  rated for teens.

If you install the app from Google Play, Google's own terms and privacy policy cover the store
and the download.
