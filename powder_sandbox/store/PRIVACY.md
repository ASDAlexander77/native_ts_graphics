# Powder Sandbox: privacy policy

Effective 5 November 2026.

Powder Sandbox is a game that runs entirely on your device.

- **No data collected.** The app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal or
  device information. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting of
  its own.
- **No internet.** The app does not ask for the internet permission and makes no network
  connections.
- **No permissions.** The app asks for no Android permissions. It reads the gravity sensor to tilt
  the simulation when you choose tilt gravity; that reading stays on the device.
- **What stays on your device.** Your saved creation, your best stars for the levels and the
  graphics quality chosen for your device are kept in the app's private storage. They are removed
  when you uninstall the app, and are not backed up.

If you install the app from Google Play, Google's own terms and privacy policy cover the store
and the download.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ASDAlexander77/native_ts_graphics/issues.
